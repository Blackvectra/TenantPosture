# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, is expected to help keep it a place where people can do good work and
be treated with respect, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept review feedback plainly and without hostility.
- Be accurate. In a security assessment tool, a confident wrong statement does
  real harm; saying "I have not verified this" is welcome.
- Protect other people's data. Never post a client's or tenant's information,
  account names or results files.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and when someone is representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to `support@nextlayersec.dev`. Reports are read by
the maintainers and kept confidential. Do not use a public issue for a conduct
report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may restrict or ban a participant for
behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. A decision is
explained to the person it concerns.
